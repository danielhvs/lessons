The kitchen: dpkg cooks, apt runs the floor

dpkg is a chef who cooks only the crates handed to them. apt is the waiter who reads the menu, orders from the supplier and hands crates over in the right order. Messages are real dpkg/apt output for fortune-mod on Ubuntu 24.04.

  • chef = dpkg
  • waiter = apt
  • truck = repositories
  • menu board = /var/lib/apt/lists
  • ledger = /var/lib/dpkg/status
  • table = files on disk
